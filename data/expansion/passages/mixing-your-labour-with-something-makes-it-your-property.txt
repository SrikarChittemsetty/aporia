The earth was given to mankind in common, yet no man ever dined in common. He that gathers the acorns beneath the oak, or fills his pitcher at the running stream, has taken what lay open to every man and made it his own; and if this be denied him, he must starve with plenty all about him, waiting upon the consent of the whole world. What conferred the title? Not that consent, which was never asked and never could be had, but that he joined to the fruit somewhat that was already and unquestionably his: the toil of his hands, the sweat of his brow. This has put a distinction upon it, and severed it from the common stock.
