Men being by nature free, equal, and independent, no one can be put out of that estate and subjected to the political power of another but by his own agreement. Whence, then, has any man authority over me? Not from the sword; for then every robber who has the strength of ten were my lawful prince, and I should owe him the obedience I owe a magistrate. Not from my birth; for I came into the world with no man's mark set upon me. There remains only compact: that men, weary of being every one judge in his own cause, join themselves into one body and set an umpire over their heads by their own act.
