But grant that the cause must be proportioned to the effect, and observe what follows. The effect before us is bounded upon every side: a world of measured seas and a measured span of years, marred in this place and patched in that, with parts that fail and parts that were plainly mended after failing. From a pair of scales I may pronounce the weight in the covered dish equal to the weight in the open one; I can never pronounce it a hundredfold greater. A limited work bears witness to no more power than the work demanded, nor to more skill, nor to more benevolence; whatever we heap on beyond that measure we heap on out of flattery, not from evidence.
