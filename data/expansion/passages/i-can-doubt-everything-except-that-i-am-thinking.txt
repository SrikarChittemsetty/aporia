I will suppose, then, not a true God, but some malignant spirit of the utmost cunning, who has bent all his powers to deceiving me: that the heavens, the earth, the colours, shapes and sounds are but delusions of dreams laid as snares for my credulity; that I have no hands, no eyes, no flesh, but falsely believe I possess all these. Let him cheat me as much as he can, he shall never bring it about that I am nothing while I judge that I am something. And so, all things weighed, this must at last be set down as certain: as often as I put it forward, or conceive it in my mind, it is necessarily true.
