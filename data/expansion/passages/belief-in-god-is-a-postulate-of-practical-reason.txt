The law within commands me to promote that highest good wherein worthiness and well-being stand in exact proportion. Yet I look upon nature and find her wholly indifferent: the just man is broken on the wheel while the deceiver dies quietly in his bed among his heirs. Nature, obeying her own laws, knows nothing of desert. If, then, I am commanded to pursue an end, and that end is possible only through an author of nature at once holy and almighty, I am bound, not indeed to demonstrate such a being as the astronomer demonstrates an eclipse, but to assume him as the very condition of my obedience.
