If motion ever answers motion in a fixed chain, and cause springs from cause in an unbroken order, whence comes that liberty in living creatures whereby we wrest ourselves from the course laid down? Mark the racehorses at the barrier: the gates fly open, yet the limbs do not burst forth so swiftly as the mind desires, for the whole mass of the body must first be stirred. The seeds of things therefore swerve a little, at no fixed place and at no fixed hour, enough to snap the decrees of fate, lest cause follow cause from infinity; else nature had never brought forth anything but what was already old.
