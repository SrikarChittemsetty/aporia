Whence, then, do they come? Not out of parchments, nor from the hand of any prince; for what a charter bestows a charter may resume, and that which is held at another's pleasure is no right at all, but an indulgence. Trace the descent of any man far enough back and you arrive at his origin, where he stood in the same relation to his Maker as every other man then living; and there, and not in any muniment room, lies his title. The great charters created no liberties. They were a recovery of what had been wrested away, as a man recovers stolen goods, and does not thank the thief for the present of them.
