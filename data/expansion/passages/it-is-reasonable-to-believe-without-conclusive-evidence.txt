To such creatures as we are, probability is the very guide of life. The husbandman commits his seed to the ground in autumn upon no demonstration that the spring shall follow; the mariner trusts a chart drawn by hands he never saw. Were we to withhold our assent until a matter stood as plain as that twice two are four, we should neither sow, nor traffic, nor keep faith with any man, but perish in the very act of deliberating. We are not given the light of noon in these things, but a lantern sufficient for the next few paces; and he who will not walk by it must stand still for ever.
