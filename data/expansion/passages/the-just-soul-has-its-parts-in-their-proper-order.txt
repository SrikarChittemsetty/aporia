Consider now the city written small. As the state stands well when the guardians deliberate, the auxiliaries defend, and the craftsmen supply, each minding his own business and meddling in no other's, so it is within a single man. There is in him a reasoning part, a spirited part, and a many-headed appetitive herd. When the reasoning part rules, and the spirited part stands as its ally in arms, and the appetites are governed as a farmer governs his beasts — fed, watered, yet never suffered to trample the sown field — then the man is at concord with himself, one instead of many, and fit for every business he takes in hand.
