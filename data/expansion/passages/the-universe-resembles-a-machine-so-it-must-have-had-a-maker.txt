Look round this world: it is nothing but one great engine, subdivided into an infinite number of lesser engines, which again admit of subdivisions beyond what the senses can trace. The wheels of a mill, the springs and pinions of a watch, the pump that draws water for a garden — all these we know to be the issue of contrivance; and the curious adapting of means to ends throughout the whole of nature resembles them exactly, though it exceeds them as far as the labour of a nation exceeds the toy of a child. Like effects prove like causes; and by that rule alone the Author of nature must bear some resemblance to the mind of man.
