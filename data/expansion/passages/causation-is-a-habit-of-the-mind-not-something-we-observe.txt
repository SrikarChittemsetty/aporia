Let a man of the acutest understanding be brought suddenly into the world; he shall never discover, by examining a billiard ball, that its stroke will move another. He sees the one approach, the other fly off; he sees it a second time, a hundredth, a thousandth. What has he learned in the thousandth instance that he had not in the first? Nothing in the objects: the impulse is the same, and no tie or bond appears between them. The alteration is wholly in himself. Custom has so wrought upon his fancy that on the appearance of the one he cannot forbear expecting the other, and this determination of his thought he mistakes for something lodged in the balls.
