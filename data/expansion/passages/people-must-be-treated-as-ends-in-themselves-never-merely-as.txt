Whatever is offered in the market has a price, and what has a price admits of an equivalent: so much corn may be set against so much silver. But a rational being falls under no such reckoning. He admits of no equivalent, and that which admits of none has not price but dignity. Hence the tradesman who cheats his customer, and the man who extracts a promise he knows he cannot keep, employ another as one employs a hammer or a draught-horse; for the person so used could not himself share in the purpose to which he is turned. A will is not to be made the mere instrument of a design it could never make its own.
