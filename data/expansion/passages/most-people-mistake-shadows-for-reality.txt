Picture men dwelling from childhood in a chamber under the earth, their necks and legs so fettered that they can look only at the wall before them. Behind them a fire burns, and between the fire and the prisoners runs a low wall, along which men carry figures of beasts and vessels of stone and wood. Of all this the prisoners see nothing but the images the fire throws upon the wall in front of them. When they name what passes, they are naming outlines only; and if one shape follows another in a settled order, they reckon it wisdom to foretell the next. Such men would take the flickering forms upon the stone for the whole of what is.
