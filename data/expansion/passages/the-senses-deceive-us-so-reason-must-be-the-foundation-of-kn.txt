A tower seen across the plain appears round, and when I come near I find it square; the oar dipped in the river seems broken and is whole; in sleep I have walked in gardens that were nowhere at all. Whoever has once been cheated by a servant is prudent never again to trust him wholly, and these messengers have cheated me often. Nor may I appeal from one of them to another, for they are all of the same household. That the tower has its sides, that a figure keeps its nature whether I wake or dream — this I do not behold with the eye but perceive with the mind alone; and there only may a foundation be laid.
