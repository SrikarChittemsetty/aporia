Ask any man who has known both the pleasures of the tavern and the pleasures of study which of the two he would consent to surrender for ever. Few who possess the higher faculties would sink into the condition of the swine, though the swine be filled and they themselves go hungry. The reason is not that the beast enjoys less, but that a creature furnished with mind, affection and conscience requires more to satisfy him, and knows himself capable of more. No man of feeling would barter his understanding for the contentment of a fool, not for all the fool's ease; and the verdict of such judges, who have drunk from both cups, is the only verdict we may take.
