Nature has placed mankind under two sovereign masters, and by their measure alone can any deed be weighed. Ask not what custom applauds, nor what a man's inward sentiment whispers, for consciences differ as tastes in wine differ, and each calls its own relish the law of heaven. Ask rather what the deed adds to the stock of enjoyment and what it takes away; among how many; how certainly; how long. Reckon every man for one, and no man for more than one, be he prince or porter. That course which, when the whole account is cast up, leaves the greater balance of good over suffering to all whom it touches, is the course that ought to be chosen.
