That bread has nourished me hitherto I know from having eaten it; that the loaf now set before me will nourish and not destroy is quite another matter. There is no contradiction in supposing that the sun shall not rise tomorrow, nor that fire shall this once freeze the hand rather than burn it; the mind frames the one image as easily as the other, and whatever may be distinctly conceived can never be demonstrated impossible. Nor will it serve to answer that things have always fallen out so, for that is the very point in question, and to prove it by the success of former trials is to take for granted the thing we set out to establish.
