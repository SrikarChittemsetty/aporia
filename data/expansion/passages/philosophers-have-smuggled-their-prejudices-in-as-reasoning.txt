They come forward with the cold demeanour of men who have followed an argument wherever it led, as a hound follows a scent; and all the while there walks behind them the conclusion they had settled upon before ever the first premise was set down. A wish of the heart, sifted and made pale, is baptised afterwards as a deduction of pure reason. Every great system I have taken the trouble to examine is the confession of the man who built it, a memoir composed without his knowledge; his morals bear witness to what his constitution required in order to flourish. One learns more of such a thinker by asking what his stomach could digest than by weighing his proofs.
