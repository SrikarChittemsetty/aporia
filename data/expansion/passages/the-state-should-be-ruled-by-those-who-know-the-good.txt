When you go to sea, do you choose your steersman by lot, or by whichever voice among the crew is loudest? Every man aboard has his opinion of the stars, yet he alone who has studied winds and seasons and the courses of the heavens can bring the ship to harbour; and the sailors who mutiny against him and broach the stores call him a gazer at clouds and a useless fellow. So it stands with the city. To govern is a craft, as healing is a craft, and its object is that which is truly good and not what merely seems so. Until those who have looked upon the good itself hold the tiller, our cities will not rest from trouble.
