Men believe themselves free because they are conscious of their appetites and ignorant of the causes by which those appetites are stirred. A stone set flying by a hand would, had it consciousness, believe itself to fly by its own choosing; yet its whole course was written in the force that hurled it. So the infant craves the breast, the drunkard utters what afterwards he would conceal, the coward flees, each imagining some free decree of mind, while nature works in him as in the stone. Nothing whatsoever in the order of things arises save from a cause determining it to exist and to act as it does.
