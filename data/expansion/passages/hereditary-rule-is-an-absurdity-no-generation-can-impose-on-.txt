To say that a man shall govern millions because his great-grandfather was strong in battle is of a piece with saying that the office of the physician or the mariner shall descend by inheritance, as though wisdom were a chattel to be devised by will. Nature herself derides the pretension, setting now an infant, now a lunatic, in the chair of state. And what right had the men of a hundred years ago to bind those not yet born? The dead have no dominion. Every age is competent to order its own affairs, and the parchment of one generation must not be made a halter about the neck of the next.
