Consider how the cities actually stand. In each of them some party is master: in one the multitude, in another a few wealthy houses, in a third a single man. Each frames its laws to suit its own advantage, the popular assembly framing popular laws, the tyrant such as please a tyrant; and having framed them, each proclaims to the ruled that here is righteousness, and chastises whoever steps aside from it as a lawbreaker and a wicked fellow. Whatever profits the party seated in power is set up as the right; and since that party is everywhere the more powerful, the reckoning comes out plainly enough for any man who will make it.
