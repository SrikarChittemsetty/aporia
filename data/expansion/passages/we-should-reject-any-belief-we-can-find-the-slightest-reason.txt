Since I mean to build once for all upon a footing that cannot be shaken, I shall use the caution of a man who, finding one rotten apple in his basket, empties the whole upon the table and takes back only the sound. It were an endless labour to run over my opinions one by one; it is enough to overturn the foundations upon which the rest are propped. Whatever admits of the least suspicion I shall lay aside as though it had been shown plainly false, and I shall withhold assent from it as strictly as from what is manifestly untrue, until at length I meet with something firm and immovable.
