When those deputed to keep the hedge about a man's property fall instead to pulling down that hedge, they have not merely erred in their office; they have quitted it. He that is set as steward over another's vineyard, and takes to selling the vines, is no longer steward but thief, and the owner does him no wrong in turning him out of doors. Power given for the preservation of all devolves, upon such breach, to the hands that lent it; and the multitude, which is neither so foolish nor so restless as flatterers pretend, may erect anew what the false steward has laid waste.
