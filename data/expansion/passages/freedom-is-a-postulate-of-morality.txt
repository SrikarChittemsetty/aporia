I cannot see into the springs of my own conduct, nor prove from any experience that my willing is not swept along with the rest of nature's stream. Yet when the prince whose favour I might win commands me, on pain of the gallows, to bear false witness against an honest man, I do not for a moment doubt that I can refuse. I judge that I ought, and thence I know that I am able. The obligation is the ground of my conviction, not the conviction of the obligation. What speculation could never establish stands firm through the law within, which were an empty sound if the deed were impossible.
