Observe the river. Nothing hinders its water from running down to the sea, and in that sense it runs freely; yet it could not do otherwise, the bed and the weight of it being what they are. So it stands with a man. He is free when no chain, no bolt, no armed hand comes between his will and the doing of what he wills; and this is all that the word can honestly signify. That his will itself sprang from his temper, his breeding, and the objects set before him takes nothing whatever from him. Strip those causes away and his choosing would be a mere cast of the dice, which no man would own as his own act.
