Look not to the palaces of a commonwealth when you would learn its temper, but to its almshouses and its gaols. A state is a body, and a body is pronounced sound not by the strength of the arm but by the meanest member, for a whole man is undone by a gangrened toe. Where the widow is turned from the magistrate's door, where the labourer's child perishes for want while the granaries stand full, there the laws are but nets that catch the small flies and let the hornets break through. Such a nation, howsoever splendid in its fleets and its marbles, has no title to be reckoned well ordered.
