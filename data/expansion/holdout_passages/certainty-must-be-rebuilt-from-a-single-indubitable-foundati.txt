Having found that many things I once received as true were afterward discovered doubtful, and knowing that a house whose lowest stones are rotten must be pulled down entire before it can be raised again, I resolved to overturn all my former opinions once in my life and begin wholly anew. Nor need I examine each opinion singly, which were a labour without end; it suffices to shake the ground whereon they rest, for when the foundation is undermined the whole edifice falls. Yet if there be found one thing, however small, which I may know to be firm and immovable, I may hope for much: he asked only a place to stand who would move the world.
