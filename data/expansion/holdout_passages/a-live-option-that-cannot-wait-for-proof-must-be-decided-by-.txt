There are questions which will not stand still while we sift the testimony. A man upon a mountain path, caught in a place from which the single escape is a leap, must trust his powers or perish; his confidence verifies itself, and his hesitation widens the chasm beneath him. So it is wherever a forced and living choice presses upon us and the understanding, for all its scruples, returns no verdict. To hold back until certainty arrives is not to keep the scales even; it is to have chosen already, and chosen the losing side. Our passional nature must then decide the matter, since by no contrivance whatever can it be kept out of the deliberation.
