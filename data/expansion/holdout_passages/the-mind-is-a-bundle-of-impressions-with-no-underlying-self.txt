Enter as intimately as you please into what you call your self, and you shall never light upon anything but some particular perception: of warmth or cold, of light or shade, of love, of pain. The self is never caught apart from these; and in sound sleep, when the perceptions cease, we may as truly be said to be extinguished for that space. What we take for a simple and continued being is in truth a train of several perceptions succeeding one another with a rapidity we cannot follow, as the parts of a river are called one river though not a single drop remains of yesterday's water.
