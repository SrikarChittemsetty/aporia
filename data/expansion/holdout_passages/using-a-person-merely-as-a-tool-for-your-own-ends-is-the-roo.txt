Rational beings are not wares whose worth lies in their serviceableness; they subsist as ends in themselves, and may not be handled as mere implements. Whatever has a price admits of an equivalent, as one measure of corn is exchanged for another; but that which is above all price, and for which no equivalent can be offered, has dignity. Mark the man who makes a lying promise to obtain money. He plainly intends to employ another as a tool, for the lender could not possibly assent to the manner in which he is dealt with, nor make the purpose of the action his own. Here is the very spring from which every wrong flows.
