A passion ceases to be a suffering the moment we frame of it a clear and distinct conception. So long as we are tossed by causes we do not comprehend, we are like waves driven by winds we cannot see, and are rightly said to be in bondage, being determined by things without us while ignorant by what. But the mind has this power: it can order and connect the affections of the body according to the order of the understanding. And in the measure that it perceives all things to follow necessarily, it has so much the greater dominion over the affects, and is the less tormented by them.
