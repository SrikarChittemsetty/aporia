It is not as certain men profess, who say that they can put knowledge into a soul which lacks it, as though they were fitting eyes into blind sockets. Our argument shows rather that this power is present in every soul already, but is turned the wrong way and looks toward the shadows. And as the eye cannot be turned from the dark toward the light unless the whole body wheel about with it, so this instrument must be revolved together with the entire soul, away from the region of coming-to-be, until it can bear to gaze upon that which always is, and is brightest.
