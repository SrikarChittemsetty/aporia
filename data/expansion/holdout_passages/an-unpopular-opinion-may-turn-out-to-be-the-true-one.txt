The doctrine which the multitude derides is not thereby refuted, nor is the creed of the many secured by its numbers. All history is a record of notions once counted heretical and now recited by schoolboys: the motion of the earth, the roundness of the globe, the wickedness of holding men as chattels. Those who silenced them were as confident as we, and quite as sincere. To stop a man's mouth because his notion stands alone is to presume that we ourselves can never err; and it robs the age twice over, of him if he be right, and of that sharper sense of the truth which is struck out only when it grinds against falsehood.
