In nature there is nothing contingent. Whatever is has been determined to exist and to operate in a certain manner, and that which determined it was itself determined by another, and that other by another, and so without end. Men imagine themselves free because they are conscious of their appetites while ignorant of the causes which dispose them to will. A stone hurled through the air, if it could think, would suppose that it flew of its own striving, knowing its motion but not the hand that threw it; and so the drunkard believes he speaks by free decree what in truth the wine determines, and the infant thinks it freely desires the breast.
