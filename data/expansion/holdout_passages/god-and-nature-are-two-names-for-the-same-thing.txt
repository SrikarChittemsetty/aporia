Whatever exists, exists in that one infinite Being, and nothing can be or be conceived apart from it. Those who feign a deity seated beyond the world, like a prince outside his kingdom, ordering it by decrees as a joiner shapes his timber, have not considered that one substance cannot be produced by another, nor can two of infinite power stand together. The eternal Being which we call by one name when we adore it, and by another when we inquire into it, is one and the same; and the laws by which the sun warms and the stone falls are not the instruments of its will, but the very manner of its being.
