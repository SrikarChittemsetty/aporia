Whether a figure be a triangle or no depends not upon the pleasure of him that draws it; and though every geometer alive should decree otherwise, its three angles would be equal to two right ones still. So likewise with the natures of things done. Cruelty toward a child that has done no wrong is not made hateful by our calling it hateful, nor would it turn sweet if all mankind agreed to praise it. Our approbations are but the eye's report of a light that shines whether we open the eye or shut it fast; the wickedness stood there before ever a witness came to look upon it.
