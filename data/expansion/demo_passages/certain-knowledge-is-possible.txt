Let me suppose, then, that all I see is false; that memory plays me false; that this body I seem to carry about is a dream, and the heavens, the earth, the figures I trace in the sand, mere inventions of some deceiver who bends his whole cunning against me. Even so, while I am deceived I am something. Let him beguile me as much as he can, he shall never bring it about that I am nothing so long as I think that I am something. Here, then, is a rock the flood of doubting cannot wash away: so often as I pronounce it, or conceive it in my mind, it must be true.
