When I remember the flogging I got as a boy at school, it is not another's smart that I recall, as I might recall a tale told me of some stranger in a far county. He who was flogged and he who now remembers are one and the same, or else the remembrance were no remembrance at all, but a fable. Nor am I my thoughts, which come and go like travellers at an inn; the inn is not the travellers. I am that which thinks, and doubts, and resolves. What is mine cannot be that which I am: the cargo shifts every voyage, yet the ship comes home the ship that sailed.
