Ask the husbandman why he chides his servant, and not his ox, when the furrow runs crooked. He will answer that the beast could go no otherwise, but the man might have set his hand straighter. And this judgment is no invention of the schools; it is written in the market-place, in the courts, in the nurse's correction of the child. Deliberation itself were a foolish ceremony if the balance were weighted beforehand: no man takes counsel whether the sun shall rise. Yet we take counsel daily, and hold ourselves in blame after; wherefore the power of turning either way lies within us, and the deed is ours.
