The flute-player, the carpenter, the physician: each has a work, and the good of each lies in the doing of that work well. Now honour we pursue for the sake of somewhat further, and wealth is plainly an instrument only, useful as the bridle is useful to the rider and for no other cause. But that at which all things aim, which we choose always for itself and never for the sake of another, is complete without qualification, and self-sufficient, so that a life wanting nothing beside it wants nothing at all. Such an end is the soul's living and faring well according to its proper excellence.
