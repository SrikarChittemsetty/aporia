We find in the world things that are moved: the stone is warmed by the fire, the staff is stirred only because the hand stirs it. Nothing is moved save by another already in act, for the wood that is but potentially hot cannot of itself make itself hot. Yet in this we cannot go backward without end; for take away the hand, and the staff moves nothing, nor does the stone that the staff should have struck. Where there is no first, there is neither middle nor last. Therefore we must come at length to a mover moved by none; and Him we name the divine.
