Tell me, does the physician call a body healthy because the citizens have voted it so? Or is there a right ordering of the parts, the blood and the humours and the limbs each doing its own work, which stands whether the assembly approve or no? So too the soul has its parts; and there is an arrangement wherein reason rules, spirit defends, and appetite obeys, as the pilot commands, the sailors obey, and the ship comes safely into harbour. Sickness in the soul is no more a matter of custom than sickness in the flesh; and the tyrant who thinks himself blessed is a fevered man praising his own fever.
