Examine the eye, and you shall find it contrived for seeing as plainly as the telescope is contrived for seeing: a lens set at just such a distance, a humour of just such a density to bend the rays as they pass, a curtain that widens in the dusk and draws close at noon, the whole hung in its socket, oiled, and shaded with a lid. Now the maker of the glass we never doubt, though we never saw him at his bench; the fitness of the parts unto the end declares him. Shall we allow a workman to the ruder instrument, and deny one to the finer, which the other was but fashioned to imitate?
